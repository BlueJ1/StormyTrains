## Decisions

- **Railway data:** the monthly files, live-update snapshots and plan files of `piebro/deutsche-bahn-data` on Hugging Face (from July 2024), not the Bahn-Vorhersage dataset named in the brief: that data has not been made available to us by the data owners. The supervisor is aware (recorded 12 November 2026). Revision, download dates, checksums and the station panel: `frankfurt_munich/README.md`, section "Data source".
- **Origin–destination pair:** Frankfurt (Main) Hbf → München Hbf.
- **Prediction times:** 24 h, 6 h, 1 h, 20 min and 0 min before the scheduled Frankfurt departure.
- **Cancelled** means origin departure or destination arrival cancelled. A cancellation only at an intermediate stop does not count.
- **Four classes:** under 20 min, 20–59 min, 60+ min, cancelled. Exactly 20 min is in the 20–59 class.
- **Split** (12 October 2026, agreed with Qing He): by ISO week of the planned Frankfurt departure, not chronological, because we have only about two years of data. Assigned by a greedy stratified split on `severity_class` (`StratifiedGroupKFold`, seed 42) and committed in `frankfurt_munich/split_weeks.csv`. 23 of 118 weeks are the hold-out set (4,324 journeys); the other 95 are the CV set (17,507), in 5 folds. Changes over time are reported by period (before / from October 2025), not by split.
- **Hold-out set:** opened once, on 6 November 2026, for the registered finalists (Phase 9 of the modelling plan). After the corrections of 12 November, new finalists are registered from the corrected CV results and scored on it once more, with class shares and the training mean as references (Phase 10). No model, feature or configuration may be chosen on it.
- **Shared departures** (same planned Frankfurt departure and München arrival under two train numbers): keep one, from the passenger's view. If any of them completed, the departure counts as completed (often a replacement train).
- **Unusual journeys** are dropped before the split: planned travel time over 7 h (ICE 1223 and 2923), and journeys where Frankfurt or München was an additional stop. 193 journeys.
- **IC trains are kept. Negative `current_delay` values are kept.**
- **Metrics:** multi-class log loss chooses severity models, RMSE on completed journeys chooses delay models; macro-F1 is reported as well. One model per prediction time.
- **No prediction** is made for a journey while a cancellation of its Frankfurt departure or München arrival is in force (decided 12 November 2026). A cancellation holds from its issue time; a withdrawal ends it from the time the first snapshot that shows the withdrawal was requested, and the journey has prediction rows again after that. Journeys that ran after a withdrawn cancellation are flagged `cancellation_withdrawn` (59 in CV, 17 in the hold-out set). The dataset was rebuilt with this rule on 12 November 2026; before, these journeys kept every row.
- **`current_delay`** in `frankfurt_munich/data/` is the latest delay that had happened by the prediction time according to the final records. The advanced models use a second version, rebuilt from the snapshots, that requires the report to have been visible by then (`frankfurt_munich/data/advanced/history_*.parquet`). Since the rebuild of 12 November 2026 the first version no longer uses the departure from Frankfurt itself. Since 13 November it does not use the departure from Frankfurt itself either. It is missing for about half the rows from 1 h on because the archive fetched live updates only every six hours (finishing plan B1); whether the report gives one version or both (Q5) is open. Missing values: 0 plus an indicator for linear models, left missing for tree models. Not used at 24 h.
- **Weather** (17–19 October 2026): DWD observations for the latest full hour at or before the prediction time, aggregated over a fixed station list per branch (via Nürnberg or via Stuttgart). 14 features; nothing is filled. Observed weather, not forecasts. GFS forecasts are added alongside the observations, not in place of them (finishing plan Q13, 17 November 2026), so the value of each and whether measured weather adds anything once forecasts are in can be measured; see "GFS forecasts" below.
- **Recent-reliability features** may use hold-out journeys, as long as the outcome was known at the prediction time.
- **No input may follow the archive's fetch clock** (13 November 2026): live updates were fetched in blocks about six hours apart, so the network state, the corridor reliability over 3 h, the time since the last report and the change of `current_delay` between prediction times are not used as features.
- **Decisions of 12 November 2026** (all in `FINISHING_PLAN.md`, Q1–Q12): fix the network state; treat the timetable as static after 3 November 2025 as well; correct the dataset build for the selection effects R5–R8 (a journey has no prediction row while its cancellation is in force, and has one again after a withdrawal); rebuild every input and rerun all CV arms; register new finalists and evaluate them on the hold-out set; the report gives the new evaluation only and does not mention the first models, whose results are deleted; scope frozen for now; remove `arrival_delays/`, `berlin_munich_weather/` and `eda.ipynb`, keep `corridor_analysis/`; create a root README; the statement of who did what is left for later. The CV inputs in `frankfurt_munich/data/advanced/` were rebuilt from it on 13 November (coverage in `advanced_modeling/README.md`). ## Main results so far

The CV results are from the rerun on the corrected inputs (15 November 2026). The hold-out results are from the first evaluation, before the corrections; B6 replaces them.

- With inputs known at the cutoff, railway-only nested LightGBM with selected feature groups reaches a CV log loss of 0.918 at 24 h and 0.821 at 0 min (class shares 0.968 and 0.896) and an RMSE of 24.28 and 23.41 min (training mean 25.42 and 25.40).
- Recent reliability helps most among the new feature groups (17 of 20 comparisons reached the minimum gain, D12). Of the other model families compared with nested LightGBM (CatBoost, EBM, distributional models, neural networks), only CatBoost with weather reached D12, once (delay at 1 h). Averaging the top five nested LightGBM configurations reached it in 3 of 40 nested comparisons, and pooling the prediction times in 4 of 20 exploratory ones.
- Weather did not reach D12 in cross-validation: with selected feature groups the weather arm of nested LightGBM had the higher error at every horizon. On the hold-out set (first evaluation) all ten paired 95% intervals include zero.
- Forecast weather (B17, CV, linear models and boosters): forecasts did better than observations (ahead by D12 in 47 of 456 comparisons, observations in 9) but not better than railway inputs alone (4 of 264; railway ahead in 31), and neither source added to the other. Forecast extremes (1.5) reached D12 against raw forecasts in 5 of 10 comparisons.
- The first models looked much better (0.759 and 21.55 min at 0 min) because of the recorded-route inputs and, mostly, `current_delay` from the final records.

## GFS forecasts

Decided 17 November 2026 (finishing plan Q13, B13–B15; modelling plan D17, 10.8–10.9). Forecasts supplement the DWD observations. A run counts as available from its start time; NOAA's files actually arrive about 3.8 h later, so this is optimistic, and the report must say so. Features cover the hours of the planned journey. Snow enters only as forecast snow depth; `wrtr` is not used (unknown for 31% of wet rows). Where GFS and DWD wet hours are compared, an hour is wet from 0.1 mm in both. The CV forecast inputs are built (B16), and the 364 forecast runs are done and audited (B17, 20 November 2026; results in `advanced_modeling/README.md`). They ran from commit 7300d61; 356 recorded a false "(uncommitted changes)" from a bug in `cv.git_commit` (fixed), which the audit accepts for them only (`--accept-b17-dirty-flag`, group decision). Next: B6. `report/main.tex` still says forecasts were not used (lines 176, 488, 504).
