# Current data work

Updated 29 September 2026.

- `berlin_munich_weather/README.md` documents the Berlin Hbf to München Hbf dataset. It is generated by `berlin_munich_weather/make_report.py`; edit that script, not the README.
- All 26 local monthly files, July 2024 through August 2026: 27,412 dated ICE journeys. Only stops at stations in `data/stations_present_in_all_months.csv` are kept (216,519 stops), so the 3 November 2025 coverage expansion does not change the station set.
- Weather for every stop of a journey, including München Hbf, is the DWD observation for the last full UTC hour at or before the **scheduled Berlin departure**. It no longer depends on delays. Backup weather stations are used up to 50 km; anything further stays missing and is flagged (none at present).
- `berlin_munich_weather/data/journeys.parquet` holds the targets: `munich_arrival_delay_min` (computed from arrival times, because the source `delay_in_min` is the departure delay where the train continues), `journey_canceled` (Berlin departure or München arrival canceled), `severity_class` and `severe_or_canceled`.
- This is observed weather, suitable for a prediction made at departure time, not a forecast dataset. Existing railway inputs and `eda.ipynb` were left unchanged.
- `corridor_analysis/` applies the same railway-only journey definition to Hamburg Hbf → München (26,526 journeys), Frankfurt (Main) Hbf → München (22,544) and Hamburg Hbf → Frankfurt (Main) Hbf (21,557), and compares the three München corridors. See its README.
- `arrival_delays/` holds two delay-only ICE/IC datasets for density estimation: every stop arrival (4.06 M) and each run's last recorded stop (558 k). Canceled and off-panel arrivals are excluded. See its README.

# Decisions, 5–12 October 2026

- Origin–destination pair: Frankfurt (Main) Hbf → München Hbf.
- Prediction times: 24 h, 6 h, 1 h, 20 min and 0 min before the scheduled Frankfurt departure.
- Cancelled means origin departure or destination arrival cancelled. A cancellation only at an intermediate stop does not count (this is what `extract.py` already does).
- Four classes: under 20 min, 20–59 min, 60+ min, cancelled. Exactly 20 min is in the 20–59 class.
- Evaluation split (changed 12 October 2026, agreed with Qing He): random instead of chronological, because we have only about two years of data. Split by calendar day of the planned Frankfurt departure (all journeys of a day on the same side), stratified by `severity_class`, seed 42 (`frankfurt_munich/folds.py`). 20% of the days are the hold-out set (`journeys_holdout.parquet`, 4,326 journeys); the other 80% are the CV set (`journeys_cv.parquet`, 17,505), in 5 folds stored as a `fold` column. File names unchanged. Don't look at the hold-out set yet, except to check data quality. Changes over time are reported by period (before / from October 2025), not by split. Recent-reliability features may use hold-out journeys (anything known at the prediction time).
- Shared departures (same planned Frankfurt departure and München arrival under two train numbers): keep one, from the passenger's view. If any of them completed, the departure counts as completed (often a replacement train).
- Unusual journeys are dropped after that, so the whole departure goes: a planned Frankfurt–München travel time over 7 h (ICE 1223 and 2923, which go via Köln and Kassel in about 9 h), and journeys where Frankfurt or München was an additional stop (`is_additional_stop`). 193 journeys (148 before October 2025, 45 after), dropped before the split.
- IC trains are kept. Negative `current_delay` values are kept; revisit later.
- Model choice and tuning on the CV folds use multi-class log loss. Macro-F1 is reported at the end as well (also in the Google Doc). Probably one model per prediction time.
- No prediction is made for a journey whose cancellation (Frankfurt departure or München arrival) is already announced at the prediction time. Journeys whose cancellation was announced and later withdrawn (the train ran) stay in at every prediction time, flagged as `cancellation_withdrawn` (76: 67 in CV, 9 in the hold-out set). Applied in `frankfurt_munich/data/prediction_rows_*.parquet`; it needs the live-update snapshots, since the monthly files have no `cancellation_time`.
- Railway feature `current_delay`: the latest delay known at the prediction time. A train that has arrived at a stop but not yet left uses the delay accumulated at that moment. Not used at 24 h (always missing) and probably not at 6 h (95% missing in CV). From 1 h on, missing values are not set to plain 0: use 0 plus a missing indicator for linear models, and leave them missing for tree models. The files keep the missing values.
- `frankfurt_munich/` holds the modelling dataset: `journeys_cv.parquet` and `journeys_holdout.parquet` (both July 2024–September 2026), and `prediction_rows_*.parquet` with one row per journey and prediction time. Its README lists the data-quality audit and the open issues.
- Modelling, 12 October 2026: `frankfurt_munich/modeling/` is the shared CV pipeline (see its README). Models for `severity_class` (log loss, macro-F1) and `arrival_delay_min` (RMSE, completed journeys); folds from the `fold` column, no gap between training and validation (dropped with the random split); only the mean and standard deviation over folds are reported; no hyperparameter tuning yet. First models: multinomial logistic regression (L2, C=1) and linear regression. Features: calendar, planned travel time, recorded route/stops, train type, `starts_at_origin`, and `current_delay` from 1 h; `is_replacement_train` and `train_number` left out. `.venv` at the repo root, Python 3.12, `frankfurt_munich/modeling/requirements.txt`.
- Local data: the monthly files and live-update snapshots (`data/monthly_processed_data`, `data/monthly_processed_data_change`), July 2024 to September 2026, as rebuilt by the source on 6–8 October 2026; downloaded 8 October 2026 and checked against the SHA-256 sums. The files from before the rebuild are in `data/_old_pre_rebuild/` and can be deleted. Forecast arrival labels (10 before October 2025) are kept without a flag.
